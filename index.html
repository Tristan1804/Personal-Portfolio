About me

The story behind the projects: who I am, how I got here and where I am going.

Who am I

Hi I'm Tristan! I got into Information Technology because I liked figuring out how apps and websites work. What keeps me going is turning an idea into something people can actually use, and I'm still learning with every project I build.

Where art meets code

To me, art and technology are two ways of solving the same problem: making something clear, useful and good to look at. The layout, colors and spacing of an interface work like the composition of a drawing, and the code is what makes it work.

Where I'm headed

My goal is to graduate, have a stable career, continue to be able to build things and continue my passion. And lastly, a peaceful and comfortable life.
